01 — Work

Six problem statements

The same team project — 7 blocks, 3 underground parking levels, an 8-acre site and about 12,000 m³ of concrete. Each sheet asks what control, record or decision stops the next stage from going wrong.

PS 01 · of 06

Approvals, Site Layout and Project Duration

Readiness first: which approvals and NOCs gate the start, how a two-gate one-way layout supports logistics, and why the ~485-day estimate carries a monsoon margin.

Open problem statement→ PS 02 · of 06

Material Procurement, QC, Storage & Accounts System

Quality gates and a paper trail: zones A–H, Gate A in and Gate B out, and payment only when PO, GRN and invoice agree — so the system, not one person, holds the control.

Open problem statement→ PS 03 · of 06

Surveying Plan & Work Management System

Specialist where it counts, in-house where it can: two planned total-station visits and routine levelling from established benchmarks.

Open problem statement→ PS 04 · of 06

Reducing Wastage of Steel and Formwork

Waste is decided before cutting starts: a checked BBS, a 12 m cutting plan and tracked panels aim at 5% steel, 5% plywood and 10+ reuses.

Open problem statement→ PS 05 · of 06

Optimum Labour Force Through the Stages of Construction

Matching crews to stages: about 92 on average and 155 at the peak — and which assumptions behind those numbers still need testing.

Open problem statement→ PS 06 · of 06

Concrete Cube Failure: Causes, Prevention and Responsibility

Evidence before blame: 23 of 56 columns failed while all 106 footings passed with the same supplier — so the records, not assumptions, decide the cause.
