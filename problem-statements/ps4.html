07

Reflection & conclusion

My reading of PS4 is that most wastage is decided before anyone picks up a cutter. Once the BBS is checked, the 12 m cutting plan is fixed and every panel carries a number and a use count, waste becomes something that can be measured and assigned — and therefore reduced.

Observations

  • Several listed causes of steel waste (loose BBS, no cutting plan, over-ordering, uncontrolled issue) are planning and accountability failures, not workmanship ones.
  • Weekly reconciliation is what turns the 5% target into a number that can be checked.
  • For plywood, the controls are about extending life — release agent, screws or clamps, careful stripping — and recording retirement.
  • The 30-day excavation window is used productively for training and setup before the first pour.

Key takeaways

  • Prevent waste on paper first.
  • Make every bar and panel traceable.
  • Systematic reuse (10+ cycles) needs records, not just care.

Team conclusion retained: steel 12% → 5% and plywood 9% → 5% through a checked BBS, 12 m cutting optimisation, block-wise ordering and weekly reconciliation; panel tracking for 10+ reuses. Member sizes stay illustrative.
