10

Reflection & conclusion

What stands out to me in PS2 is how much of the control is built into the documents rather than into one person. An inexperienced store manager does not have to remember everything — the PO, the inspection decision, the GRN and the three-way match each act as a gate that material and money must pass.

Observations

  • Traceability starts at the gate: no PO, no entry — so every later record has something to match against.
  • The quarantine bay (Zone H) separates a doubtful load physically, which is stronger than separating it only on paper.
  • The invoice triplicate gives Accounts, the Store Manager and Security independent copies, so no single copy can be quietly altered.
  • Delegated limits and exception reporting are what actually reduce the Project Manager's involvement.

Key takeaways

  • Accountability is designed into the workflow, not left to experience.
  • Quality is checked before a GRN exists, never after.
  • A mock delivery run in the 15-day window tests the system before real material arrives.

Team conclusion retained: entry at Gate A against a PO, inspection before GRN, rejected loads to quarantine and out by Gate B, payment only after a three-way match. ANPR, weighbridge and software remain proposals.
